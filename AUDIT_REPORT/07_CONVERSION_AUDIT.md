## 7.1 Conversion funnel as built

| Stage | What exists | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Attract | Technical SEO, 4 free tools, Resource Hub | No service or location landing pages, no content hub, no GBP link |
| Interest | Premium design, 4 services, process | Abstract copy. No audience, outcomes or prices. |
| Trust | Founder photo and name on About; honest labels | **No testimonials, client logos, case-study results, review stars, guarantees, or years and projects count.** The portfolio is mostly concepts. |
| Action | Form, phone, email, WhatsApp (header, footer, floating, contact) | Too many equal CTAs with different wording ("Let's work together", "Start a project", "Request a quote", "Start a conversation", "See what is possible"). ## 7.3 Missing commercial opportunities (low build cost)

1. **Packages page with "from" prices.** For example: *Starter one-page site*, *Business site (5 pages)*, *Site + WhatsApp ordering*, *Lead-capture automation (form → Sheet → email)*. Static HTML only.
2. **Website Care Plan** (monthly): hosting help, updates, backups, small edits, analytics report. Recurring revenue with zero new tech.
3. **"Your branded version" upsell on each free tool**: "Want this with your logo, prices and WhatsApp number? From R…". Turns tool traffic into leads.
4. **Showcase the site's own enquiry system as a case study**: "Website enquiries straight into Google Sheets with reference numbers and email alerts". It is proof of the automation offer.
5. **Testimonials:** ask any past clients, including discounted or friends-and-family jobs, for 2–3 sentence quotes with permission. Add Google reviews once GBP exists.
6. **Free 15-minute website check** or "Website health check" lead magnet. Delivered manually at first.
7. **Service-specific WhatsApp prefills**, for example "Hi, I'm interested in a Business Website package".
8. **Qualifying fields:** budget range and timeline (optional) on the form.
9. **Track `generate_lead`** on confirmed form success (reference received) and mark it as a GA4 key event.

## 7.4 Competitive positioning (Phase 9)

**Market reality (Paarl / Cape Winelands / SA SMEs):** many local web designers compete on price-led WordPress or Wix packages. Large agencies compete on brand. A solo studio wins on **speed, practicality and specific SME problems**, not on breadth.

**InterSacks' genuine differentiators (from evidence in the repo):**
- Hand-coded, very fast sites (Lighthouse 99–100).
- Practical business tooling: quote, invoice, job card, WhatsApp ordering.
- Lightweight automation on tools SMEs already use (Google Sheets, Gmail, WhatsApp), as the enquiry system shows.
- Python/Django capability, for future custom software.

### Recommended service hierarchy

| Tier | Service | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead (sell first)** | **Business websites for local SMEs**, fast, mobile-first, with WhatsApp and lead capture built in | Main revenue and SEO target |
| **Lead add-on** | **WhatsApp ordering & enquiry automation** (order builder, form → Sheet → email) | The differentiator; raises average order value |
| **Recurring** | **Website Care Plan** | Predictable monthly revenue |
| Secondary | Business tools (branded quote, invoice and job-card systems) | Productised, fixed price |
| Secondary | Business process automation (Sheets/CRM/booking) | Upsell to existing clients |
| De-emphasise | Branding / logo design | Offer only as a website-bundle add-on. It is already linked to InterSacks Apparel. |
| De-emphasise | "AI & Software" / custom software / e-commerce | Keep as "on request" until there are delivered case studies. "AI" without proof reduces trust. |

**Positioning statement (suggested):**
*"InterSacks Digital builds fast, professional websites with built-in WhatsApp ordering and lead capture for small businesses in Paarl and the Cape Winelands — so enquiries turn into customers, not missed messages."*
