# Website Content Checklist

## Brand

- [ ] Approved logo files (SVG preferred)
- [ ] Brand colours and font sources
- [ ] Short brand description and tone guidance

## Business Information

- [ ] Business name, address, email, and phone number
- [ ] Trading hours and service area
- [ ] Social media and WhatsApp links

## Page Content

- [ ] Homepage headline and primary call to action
- [ ] About story and team information
- [ ] Service or product descriptions and prices where relevant
- [ ] Genuine testimonials with permission to publish
- [ ] Frequently asked questions

## Media

- [ ] Licensed photographs or approved original images
- [ ] Team headshots with names and roles
- [ ] Product images with useful filenames and alt-text notes

## Trust and Launch

- [ ] Privacy, returns, delivery, and other required policies
- [ ] Domain and hosting access
- [ ] Analytics or measurement requirements
- [ ] Final approval contact
