Contact enquiries

What the form collects

The contact form asks for your full name, email address, phone number, the service you are enquiring about, and a description of the project. A business name is optional. The form also records the page it was sent from, the date and time, how long the form was open (to help filter automated spam), the version of this notice shown beside the form, and your choice on the optional marketing box.

Why it is used

Your details are used to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote if you ask for one, and keep a record of the conversation. This processing happens because you asked InterSacks Digital to respond. Sending the form does not sign you up for marketing.

The marketing box is optional and unticked. Only if you tick it may InterSacks Digital send you occasional news and offers. You can withdraw that choice at any time by email, and an enquiry is answered whether or not the box is ticked.

How an enquiry is processed

The form sends your enquiry to a Google Apps Script web app run under the InterSacks Digital Google account. The script checks the details, saves one row in a Google Sheets spreadsheet that only that account can open, emails InterSacks Digital a notification, and emails you an acknowledgement with a reference number. That acknowledgement is not a confirmed booking. If the same enquiry is sent again within a day, it is marked as a repeat and a second acknowledgement is not sent.

You can also contact InterSacks Digital by email, telephone or WhatsApp instead of using the form. Messages sent that way are handled by those services and are used only to reply to you.

Who receives it

  • InterSacks Digital, to answer and manage your enquiry.
  • Google, which hosts Google Apps Script, Google Sheets and Gmail and processes the information on InterSacks Digital’s behalf. Google may store and process it on servers outside South Africa.

Your enquiry details are not sold, and they are not sent to Google Analytics. They are shared with anyone else only if the law requires it.

Enquiry records are kept for [OWNER TO CONFIRM: retention period, for example 24 months after the last contact] and then deleted, unless the enquiry became a project and the record is needed for a longer period required by law, for example tax or accounting records. How it is protected

The spreadsheet and the script are in a Google account that is not shared with the public. The form checks and cleans the information it receives, limits how many enquiries can be sent in a short time, and does not show internal error details. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Google Analytics 4

On www.intersacks.co.za and intersacks.co.za, the public pages load Google’s tag for measurement ID G-XCN1MN0GHR. The tag loads asynchronously. It can store analytics cookies in this browser and send measurement data to Google. The data includes the page address, browser and device information, and the IP address. Google’s reports do not show that full IP address as a precise location. Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off in the tag.

This privacy page loads the same tag. The payments notice, the page-not-found page, and the greeting-card templates do not.

The purpose of the measurement is to see which pages are visited and which contact actions visitors use, so the studio can understand how the site is used.

Information the measurement includes

  • The page path, without the query string.
  • The name of a measured action.
  • The area of the page that contained the link, such as the header, footer, a service section, or the contact area.
  • The service name when the link or section is Web Solutions, Branding, Business Automation, or AI and Software.
  • Technical data the Google tag sends, including browser and device data and the IP address.
  • Analytics cookies the tag may store.

Actions that are measured

  • whatsapp_click when a WhatsApp link is clicked.
  • email_click when an email link is clicked.
  • phone_click when a telephone link is clicked.
  • quote_request_click when a link says “Request a quote” or “quote request”.
  • service_view when the heading of a service section comes into view, once for each section during that page load.
  • contact_page_view when the contact page opens.
  • generate_lead once, after the enquiry system confirms that a contact-form enquiry was saved. It records only the service chosen.

The Google tag also records an ordinary page view. A link labelled Contact or Start a project is not treated as a quote request.

Enquiry information that is not sent

The tag is not given your name, email address, telephone number, WhatsApp number, business name, enquiry reference, or the text of an enquiry.

Quote, invoice, and job card details stay in the browser. An order is passed to WhatsApp only if you choose Open WhatsApp.

Managing analytics cookies

This website does not include a control that turns Google Analytics off or blocks its cookies. You can delete cookies already stored for this site, or tell the browser not to store new ones, in the browser you are using. Blocking cookies limits what the browser keeps. It does not by itself stop the tag from sending a measurement during a visit.

  • Chrome and Edge: open Settings, then Privacy and security, then Cookies and other site data, then See all site data and permissions. Search for intersacks. Delete the stored data, or block the site from storing cookies.
  • Firefox: open Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Cookies and Site Data, then Manage Data. Search for intersacks and remove the data. The permissions control in the address bar can also block cookies for this site.
  • Safari on a Mac: open the Safari menu, then Settings, then Privacy, then Manage Website Data. Search for intersacks and remove it.
  • Safari on an iPhone or iPad: open Settings, then Apps, then Safari, then Advanced, then Website Data. On older software the Safari settings are directly under Settings. Search for intersacks and remove it.
  • Chrome on an Android phone: open the Chrome menu, then Settings, then Site settings, then All sites. Open intersacks.co.za, then clear and reset, or block cookies.

Menu names differ by browser and version. If a path above does not match, search the browser settings for cookies or website data and look for intersacks.co.za. A private browsing window usually discards cookies when you close it. The Google tag still loads during that visit.

Reading these pages does not require cookies. Sending an enquiry from the contact form does not require an analytics cookie.
