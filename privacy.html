Privacy

How this website
is measured.

InterSacks Digital uses Google Analytics 4 on the public pages of this website. This page describes the purpose of that measurement, the information it covers, and how to manage analytics cookies in your browser.

Google Analytics 4

On www.intersacks.co.za and intersacks.co.za, the public pages load Google’s tag for measurement ID G-Q85HJEHCZ7. The tag loads asynchronously. It can store analytics cookies in this browser and send measurement data to Google. The data includes the page address, browser and device information, and the IP address. Google’s reports do not show that full IP address as a precise location.

This privacy page loads the same tag. The payments notice, the page-not-found page, and the greeting-card templates do not.

The purpose of the measurement is to see which pages are visited and which contact actions visitors use, so the studio can understand how the site is used.

Information the measurement includes

  • The page path, without the query string.
  • The name of a measured action.
  • The area of the page that contained the link, such as the header, footer, a service section, or the contact area.
  • The service name when the link or section is Web Solutions, Branding, Business Automation, or AI and Software.
  • Technical data the Google tag sends, including browser and device data and the IP address.
  • Analytics cookies the tag may store.

Actions that are measured

  • whatsapp_click when a WhatsApp link is clicked.
  • email_click when an email link is clicked.
  • phone_click when a telephone link is clicked.
  • quote_request_click when a link says “Request a quote” or “quote request”.
  • service_view when the heading of a service section comes into view, once for each section during that page load.
  • contact_page_view when the contact page opens.

The Google tag also records an ordinary page view. A link labelled Contact or Start a project is not treated as a quote request.

Enquiry information that is not sent

The tag is not given your name, email address, telephone number, WhatsApp number, or the text of an enquiry. The contact form opens an email in your own email app to lesleysacks1@gmail.com. That enquiry is not sent through this website to Google Analytics.

Quote, invoice, and job card details stay in the browser. An order is passed to WhatsApp only if you choose Open WhatsApp.

Managing analytics cookies

This website does not include a control that turns Google Analytics off or blocks its cookies. You can delete cookies already stored for this site, or tell the browser not to store new ones, in the browser you are using. Blocking cookies limits what the browser keeps. It does not by itself stop the tag from sending a measurement during a visit.

  • Chrome and Edge: open Settings, then Privacy and security, then Cookies and other site data, then See all site data and permissions. Search for intersacks. Delete the stored data, or block the site from storing cookies.
  • Firefox: open Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Cookies and Site Data, then Manage Data. Search for intersacks and remove the data. The permissions control in the address bar can also block cookies for this site.
  • Safari on a Mac: open the Safari menu, then Settings, then Privacy, then Manage Website Data. Search for intersacks and remove it.
  • Safari on an iPhone or iPad: open Settings, then Apps, then Safari, then Advanced, then Website Data. On older software the Safari settings are directly under Settings. Search for intersacks and remove it.
  • Chrome on an Android phone: open the Chrome menu, then Settings, then Site settings, then All sites. Open intersacks.co.za, then clear and reset, or block cookies.

Menu names differ by browser and version. If a path above does not match, search the browser settings for cookies or website data and look for intersacks.co.za. A private browsing window usually discards cookies when you close it. The Google tag still loads during that visit.

Reading these pages does not require cookies. Sending an enquiry from the contact form uses your email app.
